The Staff Engineer Mindset

01
How will this fail?
Design for failure modes before they happen. Chaos engineering, fault injection, runbooks.
02
How will it scale 100×?
Partitioning, sharding, caching, async processing. Never build for today only.
03
How much will it cost?
FinOps at every decision. Reserved capacity, lifecycle policies, right-sizing.
04
How will it be monitored?
Observability first. Metrics, logs, traces. SLOs and alerting from day one.
05
How will it be secured?
Zero Trust, least privilege, encryption at rest and in transit. Defense in depth.
06
How will schema evolve?
Schema registry, Avro evolution, backward/forward compatibility by default.
07
How is quality enforced?
dbt tests, null/duplicate/freshness checks and data quality gates in every pipeline.
08
How does DR work?
RTO/RPO defined. Geo-replication, backup strategies, failover tested quarterly.
09
How is compliance proven?
Audit logs, lineage, GDPR/ISO27001/SOC2 controls documented and tested.
10
How can it be automated?
IaC for everything. No manual clicks in production. GitOps, Terraform, CI/CD.
