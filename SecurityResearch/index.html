How the four orgs actually hire for this

Team names and emphasis shift, but the underlying bar is consistent: demonstrated original work over credentials. Verify current team names before applying — org structures move fast.

Anthropic

Frontier Red Team and Alignment Science hire for red-teaming instinct and interpretability fluency specifically. Mechanistic interpretability work (Phase 4) is the single strongest differentiator here — it's the house specialty.

OpenAI

Preparedness and Safety Systems teams weight practical red-teaming and misuse-pattern research heavily. A published jailbreak taxonomy or novel attack class carries real weight.

Google DeepMind

AGI Safety and the AI red team pull from both research and engineering backgrounds. Reproducible, well-documented adversarial robustness work (Phase 3) maps directly onto their public research agenda.

Cisco (Talos / Robust Intelligence)

More systems- and product-security rooted. CTF pedigree, binary exploitation depth (Phase 2), and ML supply-chain security work stand out more here than pure research papers.

Capstone: by month 30–36 you should have

This is the actual portfolio a hiring committee at any of the four orgs would look for — it substitutes for the PhD credential when it's genuinely this complete.

  • 2–3 reimplemented-and-extended attack or defense pipelines, each with a public writeup
  • 1 workshop paper or arXiv preprint with early citations or community engagement
  • 1–2 competition placements (DEF CON AI Village, HackAPrompt, or equivalent)
  • An active technical blog read by people in the field, not just friends
  • 1 meaningful open-source contribution to a real AI security tool (Garak, PyRIT, ART)
  • A declared specialization: LLM red-teaming, adversarial robustness, mech-interp-for-safety, or ML supply-chain security — pick one and be known for it
