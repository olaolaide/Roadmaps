Part VII · The Scientific Method

Research Mastery & Scientific Practice

Transition from student to scientist. This is where you create knowledge.

Read → Reproduce → Critique → Extend → Discover → Publish → Impact

Literature Mastery

Start at 1 paper/week. Progress to 3–5 papers/week. Maintain a living literature review. Use reference managers (Zotero, Mendeley). Build citation networks. Track seminal vs cutting-edge.

Reproduction Discipline

Reproduce influential papers honestly. Document mismatches. Share reproductions publicly. Use reproducibility checklists. Publish negative results.

Experimental Design

Formulate falsifiable hypotheses. Design proper controls. Calculate statistical power. Pre-register experiments. Use random seeds, deterministic pipelines. Version everything.

Publication & Impact

Target venues strategically. Build reviewer relationships. Create open-source artifacts. Write for multiple audiences. Translate research into practice.

Part X · The Standard

What "God-Tier" Actually Means

When an autonomous AI agent is compromised through indirect prompt injection and attempts to exfiltrate credentials from a Kubernetes environment, you should be able to reason about the incident across every layer — and then build a defence, prove its correctness, and publish the research.

Build → Break → Understand → Defend → Prove → Measure → Publish
The Career Loop:
Learn → Implement → Teach → Research → Lead → Mentor → Legacy
One Critical Rule: Don't optimize your life around "How many technologies do I know?" Optimize around "How deeply can I understand and solve difficult problems that matter?" Someone who knows Python, PyTorch, LangChain, Kubernetes, AWS, and Burp is useful. Someone who understands algorithms, mathematics, operating systems, networks, distributed systems, security, cryptography, ML, deep learning, transformers, AI systems, adversarial ML, formal methods, and governance — and can conduct original research — is operating at a completely different level. That is the standard.
