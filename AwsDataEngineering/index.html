The Staff Engineer Mindset

01
How will this fail?
Design failure modes before they happen. Chaos engineering, fault injection, documented runbooks.
02
How will it scale 100×?
Partitioning, sharding, caching, async. S3 scales infinitely — your pipeline logic must too.
03
How much will it cost?
FinOps every decision. Spot Instances, S3 lifecycle, right-sized EMR clusters, Athena partitioning.
04
How will it be monitored?
CloudWatch, Grafana, OpenTelemetry. SLOs, alerts, and runbooks before go-live.
05
How will it be secured?
IAM least privilege, KMS encryption, VPC isolation, Lake Formation governance. Defense in depth.
06
How will schema evolve?
Schema Registry, Avro/Iceberg evolution. Backward/forward compatibility enforced in CI.
07
How is quality enforced?
Great Expectations, dbt tests, freshness/null/duplicate checks as pipeline gates.
08
How does DR work?
Cross-region replication, S3 versioning, RTO/RPO documented. DR tested, not assumed.
09
How is compliance proven?
CloudTrail audit logs, Lake Formation lineage, GDPR/HIPAA/PCI-DSS controls as code.
10
How can it be automated?
IaC everything. Terraform/CDK. No manual console clicks in production. GitOps end-to-end.
